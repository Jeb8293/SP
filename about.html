The Story

BUILT FROM LIVED EXPERIENCE.

Origin / Purpose

THERE HAD TO BE A BETTER WAY.

Selfless Progression began with a specific frustration. A prosthetic wearer needed an easier way to access and remove his prosthetic while driving without practically undressing to do it. The solution was simple: integrate a zipper into the pant leg so the clothing works with the situation instead of complicating it.

That practical idea opened a much bigger conversation. Conventional clothing is built around assumptions about how people move, dress, sit, stand, and access their bodies. Selfless Progression asks what happens when those assumptions are replaced by curiosity, empathy, and better design.
