The Story

There has to be a better way.

Origin / Purpose

Built from lived experience, not a boardroom brainstorm.

Selfless Progression began with a very specific frustration. A prosthetic wearer needed an easier way to access and remove his prosthetic while driving without having to practically undress to do it. The idea was straightforward: integrate a zipper into the pant leg so the clothing works with the situation instead of complicating it.

That practical fix opened a much larger conversation. Conventional clothing is designed around assumptions about how people move, dress, sit, stand, and access their bodies. Selfless Progression asks what happens when those assumptions are replaced with curiosity, empathy, and better design.
